# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community.

Be respectful, discuss technical disagreements in good faith, and focus
feedback on the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, publication of private information, and deliberately disruptive
behavior are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainer contact published by the repository.
